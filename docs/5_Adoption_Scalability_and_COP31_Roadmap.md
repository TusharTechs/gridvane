## 1. Phase-by-Phase Deployment Roadmap

### Phase 1 (Oct – Nov 2026): UN COP31 Antalya Live Digital Twin Showcase
- Showcase the **Antalya Coastal 33-Bus Feeder Twin** and **Ankara Eco-Industrial Twin** at **UN COP31 (November 10–21, 2026, Antalya, Türkiye)**.
- Provide Turkish industrial exporters and DSOs (AEDAŞ, Başkent EDAŞ) with automated **TSRS / IFRS S2 Physical Climate Risk Disclosures** and **EU CBAM Scope 2** proof packs anchored via `GridVaneSettlement.sol`.

### Phase 2 (Q1 – Q2 2027): IEEE P3271.01 & Estfeed / Smart-Meter Gateway Pilot
- Integrate GridVane's **NIST FIPS 204 `ML-DSA-65`** and **`PP-PQB` range commitment** SDK with DLMS/COSEM smart-meter gateways and Estonia's **Estfeed** DSO data-exchange platform.
- Submit reference implementation feedback to the **IEEE P3271.01 Working Group** on Recurring DLT Transactions for VPP state-channel settlement.

### Phase 3 (Q3 2027 – 2028): Multi-DSO Rollout Across Mediterranean, Baltic & CAISO Grids
- Scale across 1,000+ distribution feeders via multi-cloud enterprise orchestration (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure + EVM Layer-2 rollups), supporting 100,000+ prosumer EV/solar nodes with sub-second Kirchhoff residual verification.

- **IEEE Standards:** IEEE 33-Bus Distribution Benchmark, IEEE Std C57.91-2011 (Transformer Thermal Loading), IEEE P3271.01 (Recurring DLT Transactions), IEEE 2030.5 (Smart Energy Profile).
- **Cryptographic Standards:** NIST FIPS 204 (`ML-DSA-65` Lattice Signatures), Ethereum EIP-712, `PP-PQB` Privacy Range Commitments.
- **Climate & Accounting Standards:** TSRS / IFRS S2, EU CBAM (Reg. 2023/1773), I-REC / YEK-G / EU Guarantees of Origin, Verra VCS / Gold Standard, and UNFCCC Paris Agreement Article 6.2 ITMOs.
